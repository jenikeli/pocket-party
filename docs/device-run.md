# Device runs still to do

## m11: Kartwheel names, phone colour, new kart controls

Server on the Termux phone or the laptop with the cert (`CERT_PATH=certs/cert.pem KEY_PATH=certs/key.pem npm start`), the iPad as host, an iPhone and an Android phone (a third phone helps for step 3). After pulling, reload every phone: they keep the old controller code otherwise.

1. **Names.** On the iPad's game picker the games read **Kartwheel** and **Kartwheel Mini**. Play Kartwheel, Start race on Palm Bay Airport: the start gate, the grandstand banner and the padded wall read KARTWHEEL; the sponsor boards read TORQUE OIL, LUGNUT TIRE SERVICE, KWTV, CORAL COVE AQUARIUM and so on. The course picture on the setup screen shows KARTWHEEL on the gate.
2. **Your colour on the phone.** Join from both phones, one with a name and one with the name left empty. Each phone's top line shows a ringed dot in its colour and its name; the nameless one is called by its colour ("Blue") on the phone and on the iPad's row.
3. **Tilt is the default** (new players: join from both phones after reloading the host's game). The setup screen has Tilt picked, Auto-accelerate off, and the line under Steering says "Your next tap asks for the motion sensor, to steer by tilting." iPhone: tap Ready, and Safari asks for motion access; Allow, and in the race tilting steers. Deny (on another iPhone, or after clearing the site's data): the phone switches to Stick and says "Tilt is not available: permission denied. Using the stick." Android: the first tap turns tilt on with no prompt. Over plain HTTP (`npm start` without the cert) the phone shows Stick at once with "not supported (is the page on https?)".
4. **Picking a colour** (setup screen, portrait and landscape on both phones). The Colour row shows twelve swatches in two rows of 6, each easy to hit with a thumb, on a small phone too; the last three are Brown, Grey and White (Bruno, Rhino and Wisp in Kartwheel, who now can be a player's kart). Note whether Grey reads as "away" next to a dropped player's greyed row. Tap a free one: the iPad's row dot, the phone's top-line dot and the other phones' swatches all change at once (the old colour frees up). A colour another phone holds shows as a hollow ring with the holder's initial and does nothing when tapped. In Kartwheel the line under the swatches says which driver the colour gives ("Red: you drive Blaze"), and that driver is the kart you get in the race.
5. **Left-handed** (Options on the setup screen). Turn it on, start a race, hold the phone sideways: the steer area is under the right thumb, Go and Brake under the left, the status column in the middle. The iPad's row reads "… · Left-handed". Check both turned (portrait page held sideways) and on a landscape page, on both phones.
6. **Floating stick** (Steering: Stick). Put the thumb down anywhere in the steer area: the ring and knob appear under it and steer from there; they go when you let go; the thin bar along the top shows how far you steer. Try near each edge of the area, on the iPhone and the Android phone.
7. **Double tap to drift** (Kartwheel, Stick, a straight then a corner). Hold Go to drive; in a corner tap Go then press and hold it: the kart hops and drifts, the Go button takes the spark colours (blue, orange, purple); let go for the boost. A plain hold, or a hold, let go and press again slowly, only accelerates.
   **Tune:** `controller/kart/go-drift.js`, `DOUBLE_TAP_MS` (default 300 ms: how soon after the tap the press must start) and `SHORT_TAP_MS` (default 220 ms: how short the first tap must be). Note whether drifts get missed (raise them) or plain re-presses of Go start drifts by mistake (lower them), and the values that feel right; change the numbers, reload the phone, try again.
8. **Auto-accelerate with the stick** (Kartwheel, Stick, Auto-accelerate on). There is no Go: Drift sits where Go was, right of Brake (left of it when left-handed). The kart drives by itself; hold Drift in a corner to drift (spark colours on Drift), let go to boost.
9. **Tilt layout** (Kartwheel, Steering: Tilt, with and without Auto-accelerate). Drift fills the left side; the wheel picture, Enable tilt and Recenter sit small in the middle column, under the place, and are still easy to hit; with Auto-accelerate there is no Go. Drift's spark colours show on Drift.
10. **Leave during a race.** The race screen has one Leave button, at the top of the middle column (the shell's top line is covered, on a landscape page too). The first tap turns it red; it disarms after 3 s or a tap elsewhere; a second tap leaves, and the kart leaves the race on the iPad. Pressing Go or steering right after arming it still drives.
11. **Colour edge and chip.** During a race the controls have a thin edge in your colour and the middle column shows your dot and name above the place. Readable at a glance on both phones.
